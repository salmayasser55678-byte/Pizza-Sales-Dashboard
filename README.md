# Pizza-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive dashboard designed to analyze and monitor pizza sales performance across different categories, sizes, and time periods.
highlighting key KPIs such as total revenue, total orders, and average order value, along with sales trends, product performance, and category distribution.
 The objective of this project was to identify sales trends, best-selling products, and customer ordering patterns.
•Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): 
-​Total Revenue: $818K
-​Total Orders: 21,000
-​Total Quantity Sold: 50,000 units
-​Average Order Value: $38.31
-​Average Pizzas Per Order: 2.32

•​Core Analysis & Insights

-​Temporal Demand Trends: Identified that orders peak on Fridays and Thursdays, with the highest monthly volumes occurring in January and March.

-​Sales Segmentation: Analyzed revenue by category, discovering that the Classic Category contributes most to maximum sales.

-​Product Performance: Ranked best and worst-selling pizzas by revenue, quantity, and total orders to identify menu "stars" and underperformers.

-​Inventory & Sizing: Found that Large-sized pizzas are the primary contributors to revenue, accounting for approximately 45.89% of sales.

-​Category Breakdown: Visualized sales distribution across categories (Classic, Supreme, Chicken, and Veggie) to understand customer flavor preferences.

Tools: Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Visualization.
