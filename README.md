# Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze pizza sales performance, customer ordering behavior, product performance, and demand patterns across different time periods.

The project provides a comprehensive view of sales across pizza categories, sizes, and individual products, helping identify revenue drivers, customer preferences, and demand trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## 2. Business Problem

Pizza businesses need to understand customer ordering patterns, identify their best-performing menu items, and monitor sales trends to optimize menu offerings, inventory, and operational planning.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- How is overall pizza sales performance?
- Which days and months generate the highest order volumes?
- Which pizza categories contribute most to revenue?
- Which individual pizzas are the best and worst performers?
- Which pizza sizes generate the highest revenue?
- How do customer ordering patterns vary over time?

## 3. Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance through key KPIs.
- Identify peak ordering days and months.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across pizza categories.
- Identify best-selling and underperforming menu items.
- Analyze customer preferences across pizza sizes.
- Provide insights to support inventory planning and menu optimization.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue : $818K 
- Total Orders : 21,000 
- Total Quantity Sold : 50,000 
- Average Order Value : $38.31 
- Average Pizzas Per Order : 2.32 

## 5. Analysis & Key Findings

### Temporal Demand Analysis
- Analyzed order trends across days of the week and months.
- Friday and Thursday recorded the highest order volumes.
- January and March showed the highest monthly order volumes.

### Category Performance
- Compared revenue contribution across four pizza categories:
  - Classic
  - Supreme
  - Chicken
  - Veggie
- Identified Classic as the leading category in sales contribution.

### Product Performance
- Ranked best- and worst-selling pizzas based on:
  - Revenue
  - Quantity Sold
  - Total Orders
- Identified menu items with strong and weak sales performance.

### Pizza Size Analysis
- Compared revenue contribution across different pizza sizes.
- Large pizzas account for approximately 45.89% of total revenue, making them the leading size category.

### Customer Ordering Behavior
- Analyzed average order value and average pizzas per order to understand purchasing patterns.

## 7. Business Recommendations

1. **Demand Planning:** Align staffing and inventory levels with peak ordering days, particularly Thursdays and Fridays.
2. **Seasonal Planning:** Prepare for higher demand during January and March.
3. **Menu Optimization:** Maintain the availability of popular Classic pizzas while investigating opportunities to improve weaker categories.
4. **Product Strategy:** Review underperforming pizzas to evaluate pricing, ingredients, promotion, or menu placement.
5. **Inventory Management:** Consider the strong revenue contribution of Large pizzas when planning ingredient and packaging requirements.
6. **Revenue Growth:** Explore opportunities to increase average order value through complementary products and suitable meal bundles.
